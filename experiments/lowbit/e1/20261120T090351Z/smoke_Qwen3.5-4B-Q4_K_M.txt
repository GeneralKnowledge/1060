The three most important ideas in computer science are:

1. **Algorithms** – step‑by‑step procedures that solve problems.  
2. **Data Structures** – organized ways to store and retrieve data efficiently.  
3. **Programming Languages** – formal languages that let us express algorithms to computers.

These concepts form the foundation of every computer program.

---

### 1. What is an algorithm?

An algorithm is a precise set of instructions that a computer can follow to accomplish a task.
